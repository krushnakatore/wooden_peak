## Global, cross-page elements

- **Header** — logo, primary nav (8 items above), Enquiry Basket icon + badge, "Request Catalogue" and "Get Wholesale Quote" CTAs, mobile hamburger menu.
- **Footer** — brand block + "Made in India — Crafted for the World" badge, Company links, Product Category links, Compliance links, contact details, newsletter signup, social icons.
- **Floating WhatsApp button** — persistent, bottom-right, all pages.
- **Enquiry Basket drawer** — global slide-over, accessible from the header on every page.

## Navigation rationale

The primary nav mirrors the B2B buyer's decision journey: discover the range (**Products**), evaluate depth of capability (**Custom & Private Label**, **Export Capabilities**), get a lead magnet before committing (**Catalogue**), build trust (**About Us**, **Blog**), then convert (**Contact Us**). This is a shallow, flat hierarchy (max depth 2) by design — B2B buyers doing supplier due diligence should never be more than two clicks from any capability page.
